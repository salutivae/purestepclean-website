Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Which areas does PURESTEPCLEAN serve?

We serve Chicago and its suburbs in Illinois (including Wheaton, Glen Ellyn, Naperville, Elmhurst, Downers Grove, Lombard, Villa Park, West Chicago, Carol Stream and Evanston), the Seattle metro area in Washington, and Central Florida — Orlando, Kissimmee and Winter Park.

How do I request an estimate?

Fill out the estimate form below, text or WhatsApp us at (331) 600-7040, or email hello@purestepclean.com. Tell us about the property — size, bedrooms, bathrooms, condition and what you need — and we'll reply with a clear, project-based price. No hourly billing, no surprises.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. Our team brings professional supplies and equipment to every job. New residential clients also receive our signature PURESTEPCLEAN welcome kit. If you prefer specific products in your home, just let us know and we'll use yours.

Can you set up a brand-new Airbnb property?

Yes — that's one of our specialties. We walk through and assess the space, assemble furniture, remove and dispose of old pieces, configure and stage the whole property, deep clean it, and hand it over guest-ready. After launch, we can handle your turnovers too.

How does pricing work?

We price by project, not by the hour. Your estimate is based on the actual property and scope: square footage, bedrooms and bathrooms, condition, frequency and pets. Recurring clients get priority scheduling — and referrals earn 30% off a next cleaning plus our kit.

What services do you offer?

Standard and recurring residential cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, Airbnb turnover and full property setup, home organization and resets, event cleaning, and commercial cleaning for offices and businesses.
